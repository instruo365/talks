# Community Talk Resources

This repository brings together supporting materials from **community talks, conference sessions, meetups, and other knowledge-sharing activities** involving **[Holger Imbery](https://www.linkedin.com/in/holgerimbery/) and fellow speakers, collaborators, and community contributors**.

It serves as a central place to share and maintain resources that **support, complement, or extend** the content presented during these sessions.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to:

- Provide **post-session resources** for attendees and the wider community
- Share **slides, demos, examples, and supporting materials** from talks and sessions
- Make **additional background information and references** available beyond what could be covered during a live session
- Encourage **knowledge sharing, reuse, and continuous improvement** of community content
- Recognize and make visible the **contributions of speakers, collaborators, and community members**
- Provide transparent and easy access to referenced resources

---

## Repository Structure

Content is organized **by event** and, within each event, by session or topic.

Each subfolder includes a README with information about the respective session, including the **speakers, authors, collaborators, and/or contributors**, where applicable.

Materials in this repository may originate from **[Holger Imbery](https://www.linkedin.com/in/holgerimbery/) as well as other contributors**.   
Individual contributions and authorship are identified within the relevant folders or files whenever appropriate.
